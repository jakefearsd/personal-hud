How dense should the information layout be?

For a SaaS-like refined aesthetic, how much data should we fit on the screen at once?

A

Spacious (Editorial Focus)

Generous padding, larger typography, and a single-column primary focus. Ideal for deep reading of intelligence briefings without distractions.

B

Compact (Dashboard Focus)

Tighter padding, smaller text, and multi-column grid layouts. Better for comparing metrics, prediction dashboards, and scanning multiple sources at once.

C

Hybrid (Spacious Main + Compact Sidebars)

My Recommendation. Keeps reading areas (like briefings) spacious and distraction-free, while keeping navigation, filters, and quick stats in compact side-panels.
