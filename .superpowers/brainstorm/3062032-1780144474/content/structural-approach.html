Which overall structure works best for your investment goals?

Focusing on your goal to "tilt" your retirement portfolio based on world events, here are three architectural approaches.

A

The "Event-Driven" Dashboard

Starts with major world events and news sentiment. Shows how these specific events are correlating with macro trends, leading directly to actionable "tilts" (e.g., +Defense, -Tech).

News Events ➡️ Sentiment Analysis ➡️ Macro Impact ➡️ Portfolio Tilts
B

The "Macro-First" Dashboard

Starts with the big picture: long-term and short-term macro charts (yield curves, inflation, VIX). Overlays news sentiment onto these charts, then suggests portfolio adjustments based on the macro state.

Macro Charts ➡️ News Sentiment Overlay ➡️ Portfolio Adjustments
C

The "Retirement Command Center"

A split-view dashboard focused on simulations. Compares current Macro Trends with News Sentiment side-by-side, but focuses heavily on "What-If" scenario impacts for your retirement timeline.

News Sentiment
