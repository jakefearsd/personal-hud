# Investments Section Overhaul Design

## Purpose

Transform the "Investments" dashboard into a high-end, objective intelligence terminal tailored for long-term retirement portfolio tilting. It will combine robust macro-economic data visualizations with localized, asset-specific AI news sentiment analysis. 

## Scope & Constraints

- **Objective Only:** Act strictly as a research terminal. No explicit portfolio tilt recommendations will be generated by the AI.
- **Macro Focus:** Focus strictly on long-term macro indicators and historical comparisons rather than short-term day-trading metrics.
- **UI Transformation:** Overhaul `InvestmentsView.tsx` to shift from a simple metric grid to a structured "Macro Pod" layout.

## Architecture & Layout

The dashboard will be composed of four primary "Macro Pods", stacked vertically. Each pod will contain:

1. **Data Visualization (Left/Center):** Charts or robust data tables comparing current metrics to historical baselines.
2. **Sentiment Panel (Right):** An LLM-generated narrative synthesizing current news sentiment *specific* to the data shown in the pod.

### The Four Macro Pods

1. **Economic Health Pod**
   - *Metrics:* Treasury Yield Curve (10y-2y spread), Core Inflation, broad market volatility (VIX).
2. **Liquidity & Credit Pod**
   - *Metrics:* M2 Money Supply, Central Bank Balance Sheets, High-Yield Corporate Credit Spreads.
3. **Global Flows Pod**
   - *Metrics:* US Dollar Index (DXY), Global Commodities (Oil/Gold), Emerging Market trends.
4. **Historical Valuations Pod**
   - *Metrics:* P/E ratios for major US and European indices mapped against 10-year/20-year historical percentiles.

## Backend Implementation

- **Data Integration:** Expand the existing market data fetching services to pull the new required macro indicators (M2, Yield Curves, P/E ratios, etc.).
- **Sentiment Engine:** Create specific prompt templates for each of the four pods. The engine will ingest news relevant to the pod's theme and produce a focused, objective summary of the current market sentiment explaining the underlying data movements.
- **Endpoints:** Introduce new API routes (e.g., `/api/investments/macro-pods`) to deliver the deeply structured pod data—combining the numerical arrays for the charts with the AI sentiment summaries for the panels.
